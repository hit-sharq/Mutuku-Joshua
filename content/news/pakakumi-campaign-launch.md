# Pakakumi Paid Media Campaign Launch

We have launched a multi-channel paid media campaign for **Pakakumi**, spanning Google Search, Meta, and native placements. The campaign focuses on high-intent audiences, creative testing, and disciplined budget allocation to drive qualified traffic and conversions.

## What we are doing

- Building tightly themed ad groups and keyword sets for search.
- Running creative A/B tests across Meta placements.
- Tracking conversions end-to-end with GA4 and GTM.
- Reporting weekly on ROAS, CPA, and funnel performance.

Stay tuned for performance updates and learnings.
